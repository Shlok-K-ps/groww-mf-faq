# Disclaimer

This is the exact disclaimer shown in the app, in the banner above the chat box on every page, and in the footer.

> **Facts-only. No investment advice.** Answers come from official public documents and may lag recent updates. Verify using the source link.

> Student prototype. Not affiliated with Groww.

Additional notes (repeated in the README):

- Answers are generated from official public documents of Groww Mutual Fund, AMFI and SEBI listed in [`data/sources.csv`](../data/sources.csv). The "Last updated from sources" date on each answer is the as-of date of the cited document, not the time of the question.
- Nothing here is investment advice, a recommendation, a suitability assessment or a comparison of returns. For help deciding, see the SEBI investor website (https://investor.sebi.gov.in/) or speak with a SEBI-registered investment adviser.
- Total expense ratios change monthly; the figure shown is the latest published disclosure, not a live value.
- Please do not enter personal details (PAN, Aadhaar, phone, email, OTP, folio or account numbers). Messages that look like they contain any are blocked before anything else runs, are not stored and are not logged.
- This is a student prototype and is not affiliated with Groww.
